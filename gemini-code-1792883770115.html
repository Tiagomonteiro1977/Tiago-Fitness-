Simple Food & Nutrition Guide

Daily Calorie Target: 2,400 – 2,600 kcal (Creates a safe deficit to drop love handles while keeping high energy).

Daily Protein: 175g – 190g (Builds muscle and recovers fast from 90-mile rides).

Easy Food Choices

  • Proteins: Chicken breast, lean beef, eggs, egg whites, Greek yogurt, canned tuna/salmon, whey protein.
  • Carbs (Fuel): Jasmine rice, sweet potatoes, oatmeal, whole grain tortillas, fruit, microwave rice pouches.
  • Fats (Health): Olive oil, avocado, natural peanut butter, raw nuts.

Endurance & Cardio Schedule

  • Running: 2–3 sessions per week (Keep your 5-mile sub-22 min runs and 18 mph treadmill sprint intervals). Never sprint right before heavy leg lifting!
  • Cycling: 75 to 90-mile long rides. Schedule these on weekends or dedicated non-lifting days. Always pack energy gels/electrolytes.
