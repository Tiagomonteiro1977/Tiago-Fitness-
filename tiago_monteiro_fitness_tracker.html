Simple Food & Calorie Rules

Daily Target: 2,400 – 2,600 Calories (Leaves a lean deficit to trim your waist without burning muscle).

Protein Target: 175g – 190g per day (Keeps muscle mass high and fuels fast recovery).

What to Eat

  • Proteins: Egg whites, whole eggs, chicken breast, ground turkey, lean steak, salmon, Greek yogurt, whey protein.
  • Carbs (Workout Fuel): Oatmeal, jasmine rice, sweet potato, bananas, berries. Eat carbs before & after gym!
  • Healthy Fats: Olive oil, avocado, almonds, walnuts.

Sample Daily Timing

• Breakfast: 4 egg whites + 2 whole eggs + 1/2 cup oatmeal

• Post-Gym: 1.5 scoops Whey protein + 1 banana

• Lunch: 7 oz chicken breast + 1 cup rice + green veggies

• Snack: 1 cup Greek yogurt + 10 almonds

• Dinner: 7 oz steak or salmon + large green salad with olive oil

Endurance & Cardio Integration

  • Tuesday (Interval Run): 3 Miles total on Treadmill (1 mile warmup, 6-8x 20s sprints @ 14-16mph, 1 mile cooldown).
  • Wednesday (Tempo Run): 5-Mile Outdoor Run (sub-25 min pace).
  • Saturday (Long Bike Ride): 40 to 75 Road Bike Miles. Consume electrolytes & carbs on rides over 50 miles.
  • Rule of Thumb: Never perform maximum speed sprints on heavy leg-lifting days!

Mobility & Lower Back Relief

  • Elephant Walks: 30 reps per leg before leg day to unlock tight hamstrings.
  • Couch Stretch: 2 minutes per side daily to open tight hip flexors and relieve lower back strain.
  • Right Shoulder Safety: Always do light Cable Face Pulls before chest exercises. Avoid heavy overhead pressing.
